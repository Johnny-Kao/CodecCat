# Code of conduct

We welcome people who contribute code, research, tests, documentation and issue reports, regardless of experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and focused on evidence and technical decisions.
- Critique changes and assumptions rather than personal characteristics.
- Assume good faith while allowing maintainers to ask for reproduction, reduce scope or decline proposals.
- Protect the privacy of contributors and users; do not publish personal information or confidential datasets without authorization.
- Disclose meaningful conflicts of interest and be transparent about generated code or automated submissions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam, personal attacks and deliberate misrepresentation of test results are not accepted.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content, close or lock threads, and restrict repeated bad-faith participation. Decisions are made by the current project maintainer, [@Johnny-Kao](https://github.com/Johnny-Kao).

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a GitHub Issue without personal or sensitive details. For a concern requiring confidential information, use a minimal public request for a **private contact channel**; do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

This policy applies to the repository's Issues, PRs, reviews and other official community spaces. It does not impose a guaranteed response deadline.
