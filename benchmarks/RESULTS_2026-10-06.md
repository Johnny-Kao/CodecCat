- short <=256 bytes: 4

Large-response Top-1:
- chardet 7: 388/400 = 97.00%
- CodecCat: 351/400 = 87.75%

The short and medium buckets are too small for headline conclusions.

## Interpretation

The current clean CodecCat runtime occupies a useful development Pareto point:

- accuracy is effectively level with charset-normalizer on this canonical held-out set;
- runtime is materially lower than both comparators;
- chardet 7 remains the clear accuracy target.

The next research problem is therefore **generalization and release-model accuracy**, not more P-stage micro-optimization.

No release claim should be made until one frozen release model is evaluated against a new independent holdout.
